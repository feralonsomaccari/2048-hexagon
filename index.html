2048 Hexagon - a hexagonal twist on the classic puzzle

2048 Hexagon is a free browser puzzle game. It keeps the rule that made the original 2048 famous: merge two equal tiles into one tile of double the value. It moves the whole board onto a hexagon. A square grid gives you four directions. A hexagon gives you six. That one change rewrites how you plan every move.

The game needs no account and no download. Your progress is saved in your browser, so you can close the tab and continue later.

How to play

Slide the whole board in one of six directions. Every tile moves as far as it can. When two tiles with the same number meet, they merge into one tile with double the value. After every move a new tile appears. The game ends when no move can change the board.

Controls

Input Action
Q W E A S D Move the board in the six hexagonal directions.
Arrow keys An alternative to the letter keys.
Swipe On a phone or tablet, swipe in any of the six directions.
