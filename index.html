2048 Hexagon — Free Online Hex Puzzle Game

2048 Hexagon is a free, browser-based puzzle game — a hexagonal twist on the classic 2048. Instead of a 4×4 square grid, tiles live on a hexagonal board, giving you six directions to slide instead of four. The challenge is the same: merge matching numbered tiles to climb toward the elusive 2048 tile.

How to play

  • Slide tiles using Q, W, E, A, S, D on the keyboard, or swipe on touch devices.
  • When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one tile with double the value.
  • A new tile spawns after every move. The game ends when no moves are possible.
  • Reach the 2048 tile to win — but you can keep merging for higher scores.

Features

  • Free to play in your browser — no download, no signup, no ads.
  • Choose between Small and Normal difficulty.
  • Works on desktop and mobile.
  • Built with React and TypeScript — open source on GitHub.
